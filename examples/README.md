# Examples

Pedagogical fixtures that demonstrate correct artifact shape and quality expectations.

## Purpose

Examples teach format, naming, and review discipline. They are **non-authoritative for any live novel’s canon**.

## What belongs here

- A minimal project slice showing brief → outline stub → chapter stub → review stub relationships
- Short sci-fi continuity or research snippets that illustrate good constraint writing
- Clearly marked anti-examples (`BAD:`) when teaching failure modes

## What does not belong here

- The operator’s real manuscript
- Secrets, private reader notes, or publisher materials
- Huge binary dumps

## Usage

Study examples, then create real artifacts under `book/` from templates. Never copy example canon facts into a live book unless you intentionally adopt them as your own story content.
